# Tell me what I need to know about https://stackit.com/en that we know, informally, as LIDL cloud

**STACKIT** is the official cloud and colocation brand of **Schwarz Digits**—the digital and IT division of the **Schwarz Group**, which is the retail giant behind **Lidl** and **Kaufland**.

Born out of the massive internal infrastructure needs of Europe's largest retail group, STACKIT has evolved into a fully commercialized public cloud provider aiming to position itself as a "European Hyperscaler" alternative to US tech giants.

### Key Aspects to Know

* **Roots & Independence:** It was originally developed to make Schwarz Group companies independent of external US and Asian cloud providers. Because it belongs to a massive, privately held retail conglomerate rather than venture capitalists, it boasts strong economic and political stability.
* **Focus on Digital Sovereignty:** STACKIT heavily emphasizes strict European data protection standards (GDPR compliance, ISO 27001, C5 certification) and data residency. Data is hosted exclusively within high-performance data centers located in Europe (primarily Germany and Austria), engineered to protect enterprise and public-sector data from foreign surveillance laws (like the US CLOUD Act).
* **Comprehensive Service Portfolio:** Despite starting as an internal private cloud, it has matured into a robust enterprise-grade public cloud platform offering a wide array of modern services:
* **Compute & Containers:** Scalable Virtual Machines, GPU instances, and managed Kubernetes via the *STACKIT Kubernetes Engine (SKE)*, including *Confidential Computing* options for ultra-sensitive workloads.
* **Databases & Storage:** Fully managed database services (*PostgreSQL Flex*, *MongoDB Flex*, *MariaDB*, *SQLServer Flex*), object/block/file storage, and audit-proof archiving.
* **Data & AI:** Managed tools for data lakehouses (*Dremio*), machine learning pipelines (*MLflow / AI Model Experiments*), and generative AI model hosting (*AI Model Serving*).
* **Networking & Security:** Unified firewalls, load balancers, DNS, CDNs, and integrations with enterprise cybersecurity frameworks (such as Zscaler zero-trust architectures).


* **Target Markets:** Beyond internal retail operations, STACKIT actively targets **the public sector, healthcare, manufacturing, and financial services** (offering DORA-compliant IT infrastructure) as well as corporate enterprises looking to run mission-critical enterprise workloads—such as *RISE with SAP on STACKIT*.
