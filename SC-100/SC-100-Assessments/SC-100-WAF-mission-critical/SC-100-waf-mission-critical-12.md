# How is data variety factored into the application design?
The data model, data types, data relationships, and intended query model will strongly affect data platform decisions.

##  Options 01
Individual microservices use distinct data stores optimized for their scenario.

The nature of datasets considered by the application can vary, from unstructured content 
such as images and videos, or more structured files such as CSV and Parquet. 
Composite application workloads will typically have distinct datasets and associated requirements.

##  Options 02

Besides platform capabilities, programming languages and SDKs should also support the required capabilities.
